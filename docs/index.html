Stremio Kai 7M

A modern Stremio client for Samsung TVs, Android and Google TV, iPhone and Windows. Movies, series, anime, manga, comics, books and live TV, in English, Arabic and Portuguese.

Download

Samsung TV

Tizen 3.0 to 8.0, hardware player, embedded audio and subtitles.

Install guide

Android & Google TV

One APK for phones, tablets and TVs (TCL, Hisense, Sony, Xiaomi). Works with the TV remote.

Stremio-Kai-7M-android.apk

iPhone & iPad

Install with SideStore or AltStore. It is signed with your own Apple ID.

Stremio-Kai-7M-ios-unsigned.ipa

Windows

Portable app: download and run. F11 for full screen.

Windows .exe

What is inside

  • Home with collections, Top 10, continue watching, new episodes
  • Anime hub with seasonal art, skip intro/outro, anime ↔ manga link
  • Manga reader made for a TV remote (zoom, chapters, saved place) with your own Suwayomi server
  • Comics and free books with an OLED text reader
  • Live TV from your own M3U playlists
  • Profiles, PIN, themes, 12/24-hour clock, phone remote and QR sign-in

What it does not contain

No movies, channels, manga or sources. Streams come from the add-ons of your own Stremio account, manga from your own server, live TV from your own playlists. Not affiliated with Stremio or Samsung. GPL-3.0.
